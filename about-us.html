About Elek

The name “Elek” draws its inspiration from the etymology of ‘elektron’, a Phoenician word ‘elēkrŏn’ meaning ‘shining light.’

Our Commitment

Much like the concept our name embodies, we aspire for Elek Solar to be a beacon of radiant energy and innovation in the solar industry. ‘Elek’ is not just a name, it’s a testament to our commitment to illuminate homes and businesses with the power of the sun. This ethos is manifested in every solar panel we install and every customer relationship we cultivate. We aim to be a shining example of what solar energy can provide – clean, efficient, and sustainable power for all.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

There are many solar companies and lead generation sites that promise a lot and send a crew to your home/business that doesn’t deliver. We value integrity and follow through.

Competitive Pricing

We strongly believe that the power of the sun should be accessible to all. To make this belief a reality, we offer the most competitive pricing in the industry. Our goal is to bring solar energy to all homes and businesses, wherever the sun shines.

Exceptional Service

At Elek being service-oriented is of the utmost importance. We manage our own installation crews instead of farming out leads. This allows us to ensure top-notch quality, timeliness, and a seamless customer experience from start to finish.

Quality Equipment

The solar panels, inverters, batteries and hardware we utilize are industry-leading, guaranteeing reliable performance and durability. We stand behind our equipment and labor with a 25 year warranty that will ensure you see the return we promise.

Our Mission

Our commitment to quality is unwavering. Each installation is a testament to our dedication to perfection. We uphold a zero-tolerance policy for poor workmanship because we believe in delivering nothing less than excellence. Our clients can expect impeccable service from start to finish. Our efficiency, experience, genuine care, reputation, word of mouth, top quality equipment, fair prices, unmatched warranties, and top-notch service plans set us apart. We have dedicated service and repair teams in each market, ensuring quick response and resolutions.

Our Vision

At the core ELEK Solar has a deep and unwavering passion for solar energy and roofing services driven by a shared love for low energy bills. We are dedicated to liberating homeowners and businesses from the shackles of utility monopolies by providing top-quality roof and solar installations at fair prices. Our mission is to empower Americans to own their power, one solar panel at a time.
