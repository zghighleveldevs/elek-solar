Florida: A Solar Paradise

Florida isn’t just famous for its oranges, Disney World, or the Kennedy Space Center. It’s also an incredible place to harness solar energy. With an average of over 230 sunny days per year, Florida offers one of the best environments for solar installations.

  • Net Metering: Get credit for the excess solar energy you feed back into the grid.
  • Federal Solar Tax Credit: Don’t miss out on the 26% federal tax credit for solar installation.
  • Property Tax Exemption: In Florida, the added value of a solar system is exempt from property tax assessments.
