FAQs

Plan Questions

What is the $199 activation fee?

It’s a one-time fee charged with your first payment to get your system set up on the plan. You only pay it once.

Should I choose monthly or yearly billing?

Both include exactly the same services and the same 12-month agreement. Yearly billing costs less over the year, while monthly billing keeps each payment small.

Is there a contract? Can I cancel?

Every plan starts with a 12-month agreement. After the first year it continues month to month, and you can cancel any time after that. Just call or email us.

My installer went out of business. Can you help?

Yes. These plans were made for exactly that. We take over monitoring and care for systems installed by other companies, including installers that have closed, so you have someone to call again.

Do my panels really need maintenance?

Solar panels need very little, but dirt, pollen, storms and snow can all lower production without you noticing. Regular monitoring and checkups catch problems early, before they cost you money.

Is panel cleaning included?

Panel cleaning isn’t included in the plans. It’s available as an add-on on any plan and is charged separately. Ask us for a quote when you sign up.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed through Elek Solar’s secure checkout. We never see or store your full card details.

Which plan is right for me?

If your system is new and you mainly want to know it’s working, Solar Watch is a great fit. If you want hands-on care every year, choose Solar Care. For larger systems or the most complete coverage, choose Solar Care Plus. Not sure? Call us at (855) 954-3535.
