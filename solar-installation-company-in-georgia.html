Georgia Is Prime For Solar

Made for the sun.

Georgia is more than just peaches, Coca-Cola, and the Georgia Aquarium. It’s also a state with an excellent environment for solar energy. With average annual sun hours exceeding national rates and favorable state policies, going solar in Georgia has never been more advantageous.
