The fine print

How the solar cost calculator works

Our Solar Panel Cost Calculator is designed to provide you with an estimate of the cost and potential savings associated with installing solar panels on your property. This tool offers a straightforward way to understand the financial implications of going solar, but it’s essential to consider a few key points as you use it.

This calculator is only an approximation

While our calculator aims to provide accurate estimates based on the information you provide, it’s important to remember that it is only an approximation. We do not collect your email or property address, unlike many other sites, ensuring your privacy. However, this means the estimate does not account for specific factors that can significantly influence the final cost such as:

  • Utility Company
  • Location and Orientation of Home
  • Shading from Trees
  • Exact Energy Usage

We recommend using this tool as a preliminary guide and consulting with a solar professional for a detailed and personalized quote.

This is based on a cash purchase

The cost estimates provided by this calculator assume a cash purchase of the solar panel system. This means the calculations are based on the upfront cost of purchasing and installing the solar panels without considering financing options such as loans or leases. Paying in cash can offer the benefit of immediate ownership and the potential for higher long-term savings, but it’s important to explore all available payment options to find the best fit for your financial situation.
