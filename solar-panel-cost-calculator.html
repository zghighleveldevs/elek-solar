The fine print

How the calculator works.

Our calculator estimates the cost and potential savings of installing solar on your property. It offers a straightforward way to understand the financials of going solar, but keep a few key points in mind.

Only an approximation

While our calculator aims to be accurate, it is only an approximation. We do not collect your email or property address, unlike many other sites, ensuring your privacy. That means the estimate doesn’t account for factors that can significantly influence the final cost, such as:

  • Utility company
  • Location and orientation of home
  • Shading from trees
  • Exact energy usage

Use this tool as a preliminary guide and consult a solar professional for a detailed, personalized quote.

Based on a cash purchase

The estimates assume a cash purchase of the system: the upfront cost of purchasing and installing the panels, without financing options such as loans or leases. Paying in cash offers immediate ownership and potentially higher long-term savings, but it’s important to explore all payment options to find the best fit for your financial situation.
