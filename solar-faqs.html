Solar FAQs

Here, we’ve compiled a list of the most frequently asked questions along with our expert answers to assist you better in understanding our solar solutions and services.

18 answers

The most commonly asked solar questions

Can’t find it? Ask us directly

01What Services Does Elek Solar Provide?

Elek Solar offers a comprehensive range of solar services, including solar panel installation, maintenance, repair, solar energy consultancy and roofing solutions for both residential and commercial customers.

02How Much Does A Solar Panel System Cost?

The cost of a solar panel system can vary widely depending on the system size, installation complexity, and additional equipment required. Please contact our sales team for a customized quote based on your energy needs.

03Are There Any Financing Options Available?

Yes, Elek Solar provides various financing options, including leases, power purchase agreements (PPAs), and loan programs, to help make solar energy more affordable for our customers.

04How Long Does It Take To Install A Solar Panel System?

Typically, the installation of a solar panel system can take anywhere between 1 to 3 days, depending on the size and complexity of the system. However, the total time from consultation to installation completion may extend due to permitting and inspections.

05Do I Need To Maintain My Solar Panels Regularly?

Solar panels require minimal to no maintenance. Depending on where you live regular cleaning and occasional checks by a professional can ensure optimal performance and longevity of the system. For example, some of our installs in Arizona require more cleaning since it’s dry and dusty but Florida has so many afternoon showers they never really need to be wiped down.

06Can Solar Panels Be Installed On Any Roof Type?

Solar panels can be installed on most roof types, including composite, tile, metal, and flat roofs. However, the condition, angle, and shading of the roof can impact the efficiency of the solar panels.

07How Long Do Solar Panels Last?

The lifespan of solar panels is generally around 25-30 years. However, many solar panels continue to produce electricity beyond this period, albeit at a slightly reduced efficiency.

08What Happens During A Power Outage?

Solar systems are typically connected to the grid and will shut off during a power outage for safety reasons. However, systems equipped with battery storage can continue to provide power during outages.

09How Does Solar Impact My Property Values?

Installing solar panels can greatly increase property values, as solar is an attractive feature for homebuyers due to the reduced utility costs and environmental benefits.

10Do I Still Receive An Electricity Bill After Going Solar?

Yes, you will likely still receive a bill from your utility company. The amount will be significantly reduced, and in some cases all you may pay are the base fees because you have offset 100% of your electrical needs.

11Can I Monitor My Solar Panel Performance?

Yes, Elek Solar provides monitoring systems that allow you to track your solar panel performance and energy production in real-time through a computer or mobile device.

12Are There Any Government Incentives Or Tax Credits Available For Going Solar?

Various federal, state, and local incentives and tax credits are available for solar installations. Please consult with our solar advisors to learn about the incentives available in your area.

13What Is The Warranty On Elek Solar’s Solar Panel Systems?

Elek Solar offers competitive warranties, typically ranging from 10 to 25 years, covering performance, workmanship, and components, depending on the specific product and service.

14Can I Install Solar Panels Myself?

While it is technically possible, we strongly recommend having solar panels installed by certified professionals like Elek Solar to ensure safety, compliance with local regulations, and eligibility for warranties and incentives.

15How Does Elek Solar Handle The Permitting Process?

Elek Solar manages the entire permitting process, including the submission of all necessary documents and coordination with local authorities, to ensure a smooth and hassle-free installation for our customers.

16Is My Property Suitable For Solar?

Suitability for solar is dependent on several factors such as roof condition, shading, and orientation. Our solar consultants can perform a site assessment to determine the suitability of your property for solar installation.

17How Much Can I Save By Going Solar?

Savings from going solar can vary depending on energy consumption, system size, and local utility rates. Contact our solar advisors for a customized estimate based on your specific circumstances.

18How Can I Get Started With Elek Solar?

To get started with Elek Solar, you can contact us through our website or call our customer service line. Our team will assist you in scheduling a consultation and providing you with all the information you need.
