South Carolina: The Ideal Place For Solar Energy

South Carolina is not just known for its charming cities, historic landmarks like Fort Sumter, or beautiful beaches along the Grand Strand. It’s also an ideal place for solar energy! With over 200 sunny days per year and supportive solar policies, South Carolina is a solar haven.

  • Net Metering: SC’s net metering policy allows you to send excess electricity back to the grid, reducing your utility bill.
  • Tax Incentives: South Carolina offers various state and city tax credits for solar installations.
  • PACE Financing: Property Assessed Clean Energy (PACE) programs offer low-interest, long-term loans for solar projects.
